Information we collect

We collect your name, mobile number, optional email and stay details to manage your booking, take payment and contact you about it. If you send a room, event or general enquiry, we also store the property and request type, your message, and any preferred date or guest count you provide so the property team can follow up.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not store card details.

Messages and chat

Chat conversations with our virtual receptionist are stored to serve your booking and improve service.

Your choices

We do not sell your information. To correct or delete your details, contact the property.
