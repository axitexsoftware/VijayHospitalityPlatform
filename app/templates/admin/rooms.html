Rooms

Manage what guests see in Search

Search results use the active, approved records below; changes are not hardcoded into the search page.

  • Property name, address and contact details: Settings.
  • Room type, category description, occupancy, nightly rate, tax and publication: Categories and rates. Approve the required fields and publish the category after saving changes.
  • Room number, floor, individual description, amenities, optional room-specific rate, photos and 360° panorama: edit a room below. Photos and panoramas appear publicly only after photo approval.
  • Availability is calculated for the guest’s dates from active rooms, bookings and room blocks.

Only enter accurate property information, rates, facilities and photos. Search labels and layout are part of the website, while displayed property and room content comes from these records.
